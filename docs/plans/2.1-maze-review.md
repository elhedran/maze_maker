# 2.1 maze review

By Wren (game designer). I looked at seeds 1 to 5 and seed 48213 (24×16, the default [core tile set](/decisions/0015-tile-set.md) numbers, generated per [DR-0028](/decisions/0028-wfc-approach-for-2-1.md)), plus 48213 again with solid hedge at 6.0. This is a review, not a redesign: nothing here changes the tile set now.

## Headline

It already looks like a hedge maze, and that is a good first result. Corridors wind, turns outnumber straights, junctions come every few tiles, rooms read as places, and generation is dependable (no contradictions in 200 seeds). But as a place to walk it doesn't work yet: the player usually can't get to most of it. Reachable from the centre was 8% to 88% across six seeds, and a maze splits into 5 to 19 separate areas.

## What reads well

- **Winding corridors with hedge between them.** Turning more than running straight was the intent, and it shows. Round each corner there's a new view.
- **Rooms stand out as destinations.** Walled clearings in among the corridors give the eye somewhere to aim, which is what a landmark needs to do.
- **More hedge, more maze.** Seed 48213 went from 20% to 80% reachable when hedge went from 1.5 to 6.0. Solid hedge gives the corridors room to join up rather than closing on themselves. That fits the hedge-maze spirit, so it's the right direction to tune in.

## What doesn't

- **Sealed rooms.** Doorways are rare (0.4 against wall 1.0), so many rooms have no way in. To a player that is a building you can see and never enter, which feels like a tease, not a mystery. They also count as separate areas. (Rooms with no way in are allowed for now, per the roadmap.)
- **Rooms as long bars and closets.** Many rooms are one or two tiles deep: a wall facing a wall, with no open floor between. A 1×6 "room" is a corridor in costume, and a 1×1 closet is nothing to arrive at. Only a few rooms (seed 1's 2×2, seed 4's tall ones) feel like clearings.
- **Little closed loops.** Four corners close into a tiny ring, and short runs seal at both ends. These pockets are the most common reason for low reachability, and when the player walks one they're back where they started with nothing to show for it.
- **Where the player starts.** In seed 3 the centre is a sealed closet: 28 cells, 8%. A start like that would end the game on the first screen.

## The measures ([DR-0030](/decisions/0030-measures-for-2-1.md))

They were useful: the low numbers showed what my eye had skimmed over, and the hedge experiment showed how much one weight moves them. But reachable-from-centre is noisy, because it depends on which tile the centre happens to land on. One sealed room at the centre makes a mostly connected maze read as broken. Separate areas is a steadier signal but mixes harmless sealed rooms with lost corridor.

## Tuning ideas for later slices

1. **Raise hedge (try 3 to 6), then rebalance corridors** (2.3, Ian's tune-and-compare loop). This is the biggest lever we've seen. Watch that the maze doesn't get thin and sparse.
2. **More doorways, or a room exception that favours a door** (2.3). Doorway towards 0.7, or damp wall-against-wall so walls give way to doors. Goal: every room someone can enter, most with one or two doors.
3. **Damp wall-faces-wall across a room** (2.3, then judged in 2.4). An exception on `room_wall` floor against `room_wall` floor, so rooms get open floor inside and fewer bars and closets.
4. **Damp corner-into-corner** (2.3). A mild multiplier so tiny rings are rarer, the same way the straight-run exception made long straights rarer.
5. **Better measures in 2.5:** largest connected area as a share of walkable cells, a count of sealed rooms of their own, and the median and spread over a batch rather than one seed. 2.4's step-through should show how the closed rings form.
