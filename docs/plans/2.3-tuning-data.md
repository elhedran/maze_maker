# 2.3 tuning data

By Rowan (engineer), for Wren's tuning pass in [roadmap 2.3](/roadmap.md): opening pressure ([DR-0036](/decisions/0036-growth-from-open-endpoints.md) rule 9) side by side with tuning idea 4 from the [2.1 review](/plans/2.1-maze-review.md), damping corner-into-corner. This page only reports numbers. The call on them is Wren's ([DR-0038](/decisions/0038-editing-core-toml.md) leaves the first value to Wren).

## How it was made

- **Generator:** the tool's own single-maze path (`generate`, which now applies opening pressure) and DR-0030's measures (`count_areas`). 24 × 16, the tool's default size.
- **Seeds:** Wren's 2.1 review seeds (1 to 5 and 48213), plus 6 to 25: 26 in all. The numbers at pressure 1 match the review: seed 3 is 8% and seed 48213 is 20%.
- **Tile set:** `data/tile-sets/core.toml` as it is on the session branch, changed only in memory. The file wasn't touched.
- **Variants:**
  - **Pressure:** `opening_pressure` = 1 (today; no key), 0.75, 0.5, 0.35 and 0.25.
  - **Damping:** one new exception, `corridor_corner` passage beside `corridor_corner` passage, both ways round, at multipliers 0.5 and 0.25. It has the same shape as "Long straights are rarer than turns". Weights are unchanged. It damps every corner-to-corner join, so S-bends become rarer as well as tiny rings; the measures can't tell those two apart.
  - **Together:** pressure 0.5 plus damping 0.5.
- **To rerun:** `cargo run --release -p maze-core --example tuning` (`crates/maze-core/examples/tuning.rs`, commit 56b3257 on `rowan/2.3-pressure`).

## What stands out

Rowan's reading, for Wren to check against the renders:

- **Pressure moves both measures the right way, and steadily.** As pressure drops from 1 to 0.25, mean reachable goes from 55% to 81% and mean separate areas from 12.6 to 9.9. The worst seed improves most: the minimum goes from 1% to 58%. No variant needed a restart or failed.
- **Damping helps the mean about as much as pressure 0.5 does, but not the worst cases.** Damping 0.5 gives a mean of 69%, but its worst seed is still 14%, against 32% at pressure 0.5. Damping 0.25 brings the areas down more (9.6) but leaves a seed at 2%.
- **Together isn't better than pressure alone in this sample.** It gets fewer areas, but has the same mean reach and worse minimums.
- **One seed is noisy,** as Wren said in the 2.1 review. Reach depends on where the centre lands, and a single seed can jump from 90% to 3% between neighbouring variants. Judge by the means, the minimums and the renders, not by any one seed.
