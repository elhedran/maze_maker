# 2.3 tuning review

By Wren (game designer), for the tuning pass in [roadmap 2.3](/roadmap.md). This is a game-design call and a two-way door ([DR-0018](/decisions/0018-one-way-doors-go-to-ian.md)), approved by Wren and Marram. It sets one number. Nothing else in the tile set changes.

## What I compared

[Rowan's tuning data](/plans/2.3-tuning-data.md): 26 seeds at 24 × 16 with the [DR-0030](/decisions/0030-measures-for-2-1.md) measures. It covers opening pressure ([DR-0036](/decisions/0036-growth-from-open-endpoints.md) rule 9) at 1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.35 and 0.25; my tuning idea 4 from the [2.1 review](/plans/2.1-maze-review.md), damping corner-into-corner, at 0.5 and 0.25; and pressure 0.5 with damping 0.5 together. I also looked at the renders of seeds 1, 3 and 48213.

## By the numbers

- **Pressure does what Ian asked for, steadily.** From 1 to 0.5, mean reachable goes from 55% to 68% and the worst seed from 1% to 32%. Separate areas fall from 12.6 to 10.9. The worst case gains most, and that matters most to a player: no more mazes where you can walk almost nowhere.
- **Below 0.5 the mean keeps rising,** to 77% at 0.35 and 81% at 0.25, but the worst seed at 0.35 (31%) is no better than at 0.5. Only 0.25 lifts it, to 58%.
- **Damping helps the average but not the worst cases.** Damping 0.5 gets a mean of 69%, much like pressure 0.5, but still leaves a seed at 14%. At 0.25 one seed is at 2%. With pressure added, the minimum gets worse, down to 2%.

## By eye

- **Pressure 0.5 keeps the maze's character.** Seed 1 at 0.5 has the same winding corridors, hedge between them and walled rooms as at 1. More of it simply joins up. Seed 48213 goes from a scatter of pockets to one walkable maze.
- **What's left cut off at 0.5 is mostly sealed rooms**, not lost corridor. Pressure is the wrong lever for those. Doorways are (idea 2).
- **Damping takes away S-bends as well as tiny rings.** S-bends are part of what makes a hedge maze wind, and a turn that hides what's next is where curiosity comes from. Damping pays for its average gain with some of that.

## The value: `opening_pressure = 0.5`

- **The biggest gain where it counts.** The worst case goes from 1% to 32%, the steepest step in the whole sweep, and the maze still looks like itself.
- **It biases rather than forbids, and that is how it should feel.** At 0.5 a piece that closes a ring is still half as likely, so loops still form. Loops are good for a wanderer: a way round instead of walking back the way you came. Lower values push towards tree-shaped mazes, with fewer loops and more backtracking. Our measures can't see loops yet, and the data has no renders below 0.5, so I won't go further by eye.
- **It suits world growth (2.6) too.** In a grown world, pressure keeps more endpoints open, which widens a thin frontier. In my 2.6 walks, seed 1 opened with only two endpoints, both on one side. 0.5 is a moderate push, so it shouldn't make growth sprawl. 2.6 step 2 measures the frontier at pressure 1 and at this value, and that will tell us whether growth needs a different number.
- **It's the value Ian's 2.3 demo uses,** so the demo and the file agree.

## Damping corner-into-corner: don't add it now

Pressure beats it on the worst case. Combining the two makes the minimums worse, and damping costs the S-bends that make corridors wind. So the hand edit to `core.toml` isn't worth it. Idea 4 stays on the list, and 2.4's step-through can show whether tiny rings are still a real cause once pressure is in.

## What to revisit at 2.5

1. **Try 0.35 and 0.25 with renders** and a loop or dead-end count, to see whether the higher mean costs loops and adds backtracking.
2. **Sealed rooms:** doorway weight (idea 2) is now the bigger lever for separate areas.
3. **Better measures** (idea 5): largest area as a share of walkable cells, sealed rooms counted on their own, and the median and spread over a batch. Reachable from the centre is still noisy for a single seed: one seed swings from 90% to 3% between neighbouring variants.
4. **Grown worlds:** 2.6 step 2's frontier measure at 0.5. If grown worlds want a different pressure from the single maze, the decision is whether one key is enough.
